Methodology

How the forecast is built

  1. Capture the closing-window option chain for the nearest expiry, including OI, OI change and traded volume for every strike.
  2. Infer writer positioning per strike (long build-up, short build-up, short covering, long unwinding) and weight it by distance from spot.
  3. Combine the aggregate positioning score with price momentum and the India VIX regime to produce a gap-up / gap-down / flat label.
  4. Translate the score into a confidence percentage and an expected gap in percent of spot. Each signal is stored and graded against the next session's actual open.

Reading the columns

Basis summarises today's positioning — the OI build-up type alongside the price and OI change that produced it.

Confidence is the model's normalised score for the chosen side. A low-confidence directional call is closer to a coin flip; treat the flat bucket as the absence of an edge.

Predicted gap % is an estimate of the next session's opening gap versus today's close, not a price target.

Forecasts are model estimates derived from public OI and price data. They are not investment advice, not a recommendation to buy or sell, and past accuracy does not guarantee future results. Finance Candle is not a SEBI-registered adviser or research analyst. Each prediction is saved and graded against the next session's actual open; open a stock to inspect its track record.
