Frequently asked
Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. Cancellation is instant — you stay on Free from the next billing cycle. We don't charge for partial months and you can re-subscribe whenever you want.

What happens to my data if I downgrade?

Your stocks, AI history, and broker connections stay intact. Free has a 15-holding cap, so any holdings beyond 15 stay visible but you can't add new ones until you upgrade or remove some.

Are AI quotas per month or per day?

AI analyses are per calendar month and reset on the 1st. AI chat messages are per day and reset at midnight IST.

Do you support GST invoices?

Coming soon. After Razorpay integration goes live, every paid invoice will carry your business GSTIN if added in Settings.

Is the market data really live?

Pro & Elite get refresh every 15 seconds during market hours (09:15–15:30 IST). Free gets refresh every 5 minutes. None of the plans use exchange-licensed tick-by-tick data; the polling cadence is the most "live" you can get on REST APIs without a WebSocket subscription.
