FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is my broker data safe?
Yes. We use read-only scopes on Fyers and Dhan — we can fetch your holdings and quotes, but cannot place, modify, or cancel orders. Tokens are encrypted at rest and you can revoke access from your broker dashboard at any time.
Do I need to be an experienced trader?
Not at all. The dashboard and AI assistant work whether you have one mutual fund or 200 holdings. Advanced tools (options, swing scanners, VaR) are there when you're ready — but never in the way.
Is Finance Candle SEBI-registered? Do you give buy/sell calls?
Finance Candle is an analytics platform, not a SEBI-registered investment advisor. Our AI explanations, screeners, and signals are educational and for informational use only. Always do your own research or consult a SEBI-registered advisor before trading.
What happens after my 7-day Elite trial?
You'll automatically drop to the free Basic plan — your data stays, but Elite features lock. Upgrade to Pro (₹399/mo) or Elite (₹999/mo) any time. We don't ask for a card upfront, so there's no surprise billing.
Can I pay monthly via UPI?
Yes — UPI AutoPay, RazorPay subscriptions, and major Indian credit/debit cards are all supported. Annual plans get a 33% discount.
Do you support BSE, MCX, or US stocks?
Today we're focused on NSE cash + F&O and AMFI mutual funds — the universe most Indian retail investors actually use. BSE, MCX commodities, and US equities are on the 2026 roadmap.
